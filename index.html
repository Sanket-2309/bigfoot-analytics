What Our Audits Actually Found

Real findings from real audits — not theoretical checklists.

Credit Scoring · Art. 10(3)

Missing Data Rewarded Over Financial Merit

Absent checking account data ranked as the single most influential feature in a credit scoring model — above loan duration, credit amount, and age. The model was approving applicants because their data was missing, not because they were creditworthy.

HIGH SEVERITY
Fraud Detection · Art. 15

The Accuracy Paradox — 99.8% Means Nothing

A model predicting "legitimate" for every transaction achieves 99.83% accuracy. Our audit found the model only captured 45.2% of fraud value in euros — meaning thousands of euros in fraud escaped in the test period alone.

CRITICAL
Fraud Detection · Art. 13 + GDPR Art. 22

GDPR Conflicts Directly With EU AI Act

PCA anonymisation applied for GDPR compliance made 93.3% of model features legally unexplainable. Customers have a right to explanation under GDPR Article 22 — the anonymisation makes that right impossible to fulfil.

HIGH SEVERITY
Credit Scoring · Art. 10(2)(f)

18-Point Accuracy Gap for Young Adults

Young adults under 25 faced systematically lower approval rates and an 18-percentage-point accuracy gap compared to older applicants — not because of higher credit risk, but because of structurally shorter credit histories.

HIGH SEVERITY
Fraud Detection · Art. 15

Wrong Decision Threshold Deployed

Default threshold of 0.5 left significant fraud undetected. Our threshold analysis showed that changing to 0.3 raises F1 from 0.79 to 0.85 with near-zero increase in false positives — a simple fix with major compliance impact.

HIGH SEVERITY
Both Models · Art. 14

No Human Oversight Mechanism Documented

Neither audited system had documented override procedures, monitoring dashboards, or shutdown protocols — all mandatory under Article 14. Human oversight was assumed, not architected.
